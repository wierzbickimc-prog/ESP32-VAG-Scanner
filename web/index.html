ESP32 VAG Scanner

A KW1281 diagnostic scanner for older VW and Audi cars (about 1990–2004), built around the 2002 EuroVan: fault codes, live measuring blocks, and a four-slot dashboard. Version ….

Install

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (the install uses Web Serial).
  2. Connect the board's USB-serial port to the computer, not the port the KKL cable uses.
  3. Click Install, choose the port (on a Mac it's named usbserial), and wait about two minutes.
This browser can't install firmware. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over HTTPS to install.

Updating keeps your Wi-Fi network and dashboard layout unless you choose to erase the device.

Hardware

  • Guition JC8012P4A1C: ESP32-P4 (chip revision v1.x) with a 10.1" 800×1280 touch screen and an ESP32-C6 for Wi-Fi.
  • A KKL USB cable with an FTDI chip (0403:6001) in the board's other USB port.

Good to know

  • Nothing talks to the car until you tap a module or Scan.
  • Fault codes show their number, OBD P-code and fault type. Descriptions come from the Ross-Tech wiki: read them on the scanner over Wi-Fi, or scan the QR code with a phone.
  • On a 2001–2003 EuroVan the engine talks at 10400 baud and the 01P transmission at 1200; the scanner finds the speed by itself.
